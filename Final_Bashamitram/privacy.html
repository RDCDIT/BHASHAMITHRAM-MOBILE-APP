Privacy Policy

Bhashamithram II — Advanced Multilingual Malayalam Dictionary Application

Effective Date: September 16, 2026
Organization: C-DIT, Government of Kerala

Bhashamithram II is an authoritative Malayalam Dictionary and language-resource mobile application developed by the R&D Division of the Centre for Development of Imaging Technology (C-DIT), an autonomous institution established by the Government of Kerala.

We are deeply committed to safeguarding user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data practices, explains how the application operates, and clarifies the minimal permissions requested to deliver its features.

1. Core Privacy Commitments

🔒 100% Offline Lookups

Over 233,000 words and 551,000 definitions reside on your device. Search lookups are never sent to any remote server.

👤 No User Accounts

No registration, sign-in, or profile creation is required. You can access all dictionary resources anonymously.

🚫 Zero Ads & Tracking

No advertisements (no AdMob), no tracking SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Facebook SDKs).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Bhashamithram II does not collect, sell, rent, or harvest:

  • Personal Identifiers: Real names, email addresses, telephone numbers, home addresses.
  • Device Identification: Advertising IDs (GAID/IDFA), hardware serial numbers, or IMEIs.
  • Sensitive Data: Contacts, SMS, photos library, calendar, or precise GPS location.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Bhashamithram II requests only system permissions strictly necessary to enable user-initiated features:

A. Microphone Access (RECORD_AUDIO)

Used exclusively for on-demand Voice Search. When you tap the microphone button, your spoken audio is captured and converted into text to search the dictionary.

Privacy Safeguard: Voice audio is processed ephemerally in real time solely to return text search terms. Audio recordings are not stored permanently, not sold, and not used for behavioral profiling or model training.

B. Photos & Document Picker (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

Used solely in the "Suggest Word" (വാക്ക് നിർദ്ദേശിക്കുക) module if you voluntarily choose to attach an illustrative photo or diagram for a new word suggestion. The application accesses only the single file you explicitly select.

C. Network Access (INTERNET)

Required only for optional online capabilities:

  • Transmitting user-initiated word contributions to C-DIT editors.
  • Transmitting audio streams to speech-to-text engines during voice search.
  • Streaming online audio pronunciations when requested.

All primary dictionary searching, definitions, and word browsing function entirely without an internet connection.

4. Voluntary Word Contributions

Users may optionally suggest new words, regional dialects, or idioms to the C-DIT lexicographical review team.

  • Data Collected: The linguistic content you submit (word, meaning, grammar notes, and optional photo).
  • Purpose: Academic and linguistic review for inclusion in future dictionary revisions.
  • Transmission Security: All submissions are encrypted using TLS/HTTPS and authenticated with cryptographic HMAC signatures to protect integrity.

5. Local On-Device Storage

The app uses local device storage (AsyncStorage and SQLite) solely to retain your selected settings (e.g. font size, language mode) and local database cache version. This data never leaves your physical phone.

6. Security & Data Protection

We employ robust industry-standard safeguards to secure network communication:

  • Transport Layer Security: All API communication takes place over HTTPS/TLS (https://).
  • No Third-Party Brokers: We never share, trade, or monetize any user data with commercial brokers or advertising aggregators.

7. Children's Privacy

Bhashamithram II is designed as an educational reference tool suitable for general audiences. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, adhering to COPPA standards and Google Play Families guidelines.

8. Policy Updates

Any future modifications to this policy will be published at this URL with an updated Effective Date.
